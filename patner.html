PARTNER RULSE

Last updeted: August 22, 2026

Brand Representation & Conduct

As an official partner of our hosting platform, you represent our brand. Partners are expected to maintain professional behavior within public communities, Discord servers, and during live streams or content creation.

Content & Stream Guidelines

If you are showcasing our game servers or web hosting on your platform (such as live streams or video content), you must ensure that your content adheres to standard platform terms. Avoid promoting hate speech, exploits, or illegal activities while using our sponsored resources.

Fair Promotion Practices

Partners must clearly disclose their partnership when sharing referral links or promotional codes with their audience. Misleading claims about server performance, uptime guarantees, or unauthorized discounts are strictly prohibited.

Support and Communication

Partners are given priority access to our support channels. In return, we expect partners to report any critical bugs, server vulnerabilities, or network issues privately to our management team rather than exploiting them publicly.

Exclusivity & Competing Services

As an official partner of our hosting platform, you are strictly prohibited from promoting, advertising, or partnering with any other competing game or web hosting providers while active in our partner program. Our brand expects your exclusive promotional support within the hosting space.
