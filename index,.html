PRYVECY POLICY

Last updeted: August 22, 2026

Information We Collect

When you use our hosting services, we collect information that you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, purchase a hosting plan, or contact our support team. This may include your name, email address, billing details, and server configuration preferences.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and provide you with the best possible Minecraft and web hosting experience. This includes setting up your game servers, managing billing, sending technical notices, and providing customer support.

Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your personal information and server data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect it.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about our Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please feel free to reach out to our support team through our Discord community or customer portal.
